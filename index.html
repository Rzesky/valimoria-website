01 / THE BEGINNING

A world worth exploring.

Valimoria Online is an independent 2D action MMORPG inspired by the spirit of classic fantasy adventures.

Journey through a medieval pixel-HD world, encounter dangerous creatures, develop your character and uncover the stories of Valimoria. The game is currently in early alpha, with systems and content evolving throughout development.

EARLY ALPHA DEVELOPMENTValimoria is actively being built. Gameplay and features are subject to change.

03 / CHOOSE YOUR PATH

Four classes. One legend.

Choose how your story will unfold. Class availability will expand as development progresses.

MELEE · IN DEVELOPMENT

Warrior Wojownik

A fearless frontline fighter who relies on strength, resilience and devastating close-range attacks. Perfect for players who prefer direct combat.

MAGIC · IN DEVELOPMENT

Mage Mag

A master of arcane energy who controls the battlefield with powerful spells and elemental magic. Relies on timing and tactical positioning.

RANGED · IN DEVELOPMENT

Archer Łucznik

An agile ranged specialist who strikes from a distance, uses precise shots and keeps enemies at bay through movement and positioning.

AGILITY · IN DEVELOPMENT

Assassin Zabójca

A swift, elusive fighter specializing in daggers, ambushes and rapid attacks. Excels at exploiting enemy weaknesses.
